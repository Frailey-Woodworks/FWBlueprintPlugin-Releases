# 1.88.64 (development)

Title: Joint layout on every frame door; exact joint icons

- Door sheets: the joint-based layout now applies to every frame door, whatever its insert (mesh, louvered, louvered + mesh, solid panel).
  - Half-lap doors get the "half-lap joint" title line, the joint icon and the rail-width dimension at the right of the top rail.
  - Stile-and-rail doors get their title line, icon, stile-width dimension and exact-fit rails.
- Door sheets: the joint icons are now exact copies of the owner's drawings (rail behind the stile), placed and scaled as on his sample sheets.
- Unchanged and still insert-specific: the solid-panel note, the solid-panel CNC guide outlines, and the mesh/louvre CNC files and slats.
