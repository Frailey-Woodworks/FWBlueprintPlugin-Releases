Title Page: console picture.

- When the model has no title page yet, Title Page builds one from scratch. Until now that page had no picture of the console; the picture only appeared when an existing title page was copied.
- A page built from scratch now gets the console picture, set up like your own title pages: a parallel view from the front-left looking down, in Rendered display. It sits left of the info column and below the stock list, and is locked so it can't be panned by accident. The backdrop, lights, plants and turntable layers are hidden in that picture; your model's layers are not changed.
- If a title page already exists, it is still copied as before, picture included.
- Tested in hidden Rhino on a copy: one Rendered picture with the whole console inside the frame (about 85% fill), viewed from the front-left.
